# Gaia-Gov
Bringing ordinary people closer to their governments

This is not about electing officails. That is done somewhere else. This is about letting the elected officials know what the people want. As an example in Gia Gov there is 2 elected officials, rramsey78 and Giagov2023. The issue in question is part of the feature list, The map. 

Giagov2023 proposes that we need the map. The reason we need the map is that we have to sort people into juristictions. If a voter lives in Bangalore India, they shall be able to place their portion of consensus on issues of the city of Bangalore, the state of Karnataka, the country of India, and global issues. 

rramsey78 says we do not need this feature. Gia gov should be able to be applied to any group that has leaders, including gia gov. If you are reading this and want to participate you should be able to cast your portion of consensus on the issue of whether we need the map. The issue of which juristiction you shall be bale to cast your portion of consensus on will be decided by membership that will be administered by an administrator. For civic issues, your jusristiction will be decided by adress, but if a corporation wants to use Gia gov, then employees, for clubs, club membership etc.

We want to develop a piece of software that will allow you to tell the elected officials what they want. If you think the map feature is important give your portion of consensus to Giagov2023, and if you think it is not imporant give your portion of consensus to rramsey78. The software will have to tally the portions of consensus and before the issue will be decided on, the elected officials will be able to see who has the most consensus and hopefully that will affect their decisions.
